Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Chili flakes (optional)

Instructions

  1. Toast the bread slices until golden.
  2. Cut the avocado in half and remove the seed.
  3. Scoop the avocado into a bowl.
  4. Mash the avocado with a fork.
  5. Add lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  6. Spread the avocado mixture over the toasted bread.
  7. Add chili flakes if desired and serve fresh!

A delicious and healthy breakfast made with fresh avocado and crispy bread.
