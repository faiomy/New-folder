Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg (to mix with ricotta)
  • Marinara sauce
  • Zucchini, sliced
  • Spinach
  • Mushrooms, sliced
  • Onion and garlic

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Sauté onions, garlic, mushrooms, zucchini, and spinach until soft.
  3. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese with an egg and some herbs.
  4. Spread a layer of marinara sauce in a baking dish.
  5. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, sautéed vegetables, mozzarella, and sauce.
  6. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a generous layer of mozzarella and parmesan.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  8. Remove foil and bake for another 15-20 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden.
  9. Let it rest for 10 minutes before serving.

A delightful and satisfying vegetarian alternative to traditional lasagna.
