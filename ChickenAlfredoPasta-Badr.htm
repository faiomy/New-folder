Ingredients

  • 400g pasta (Fettuccine or Penne)
  • 2 cooked chicken breasts (sliced)
  • 1.5 cups cooking cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter, garlic, salt, and pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water, then drain.
  2. Melt butter and garlic in a pan, add cooking cream and Parmesan cheese, and stir until smooth.
  3. Add the cooked pasta and slice add chicken into the sauce, mix well, and serve hot!

A delicious and rich classic Italian pasta dish made with tender chicken and a creamy Parmesan sauce.
